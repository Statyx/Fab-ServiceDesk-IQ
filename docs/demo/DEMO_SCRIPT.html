A fictional managed-service operator, six customers, AI agents on the front line, and one question that neither the data nor the contract can answer alone. This script gives the order of the screens, the clicks, what to say and the wording for the Zava IQ part, so the audience understands the context of what they are about to see — before they see it.

1. The storyline

“Fabrikam and Litware both fell below the same 40% zero-touch XLA last week — what does each contract make Zava owe?”

The rate is calculated in Fabric, in DAX, on a Direct Lake semantic model. The clause is retrieved by the Foundry contracts agent from the Foundry IQ knowledge base. The language model routes, cites and phrases — it never calculates, and never turns a clause into an invented rule.

Customer, ISO week 38Zero-touchContractConsequence
Fabrikam Industries34.0% (was 46.0%)CTR-FAB-2026 · XLA-FAB-01A service credit of 9,250 EUR: 5% of the 185,000 EUR monthly fee, capped at 15%.
Litware Insurance38.0% (was 48.0%)CTR-LIT-2026No credit. A written remediation plan within 10 business days — due 2 October.

Behind the Fabrikam drop: a Corporate VPN update breaks remote access and Teams calls at Fabrikam Lyon. Tickets pile up, the AI agents escalate, a device-health tool fails upstream, and zero-touch falls through the XLA. The chain runs Detect → Diagnose → Act → Ground → AgentOps → Value.

Same target, same week, close numbers. Two opposite obligations. The figures say how much; the contract says what we owe; the work context says who acts. None of them is enough on its own.

2. Before the demo

The day before

  • Fabric capacity running (a paused capacity breaks everything and answers 404).
  • Offline gate green: python -m pytest tests -q and npm test in app-zava-service-desk/.
  • Activator started and Operations Agent bindings done (UI-only, see docs/DEPLOYMENT.md).
  • Full rehearsal in the browser you will use on stage, already signed in to the demo tenant.
  • Optional: re-record the prepared answers with python -m fabric.data_agent.capture_frozen_answers. A 401 means the token expired: run it again.

One hour before

  • python deploy_all.py --warmup — pay the cold start off stage.
  • Start the live stream: python -m fabric.eventhouse.inject_event --scenario vpn-lyon --loop --interval 30.
  • Open the application from its Fabric item (app_url in state.json) and sign in.
  • Ask one free question in the assistant: it wakes up the Data Agent.
  • Second tab open on /diagnostic (not in the navigation, reachable without signing in).

Five minutes before

  • Browser zoom at 100%, full-screen window, theme chosen.
  • Back to the cover; close the assistant if it still holds rehearsal answers.
  • On Zava IQ, click Reset.
  • Notifications off.

4. Zava IQ — layers and wording

Fabric IQ

The semantic model computes the zero-touch rates and the XLA credit. The ontology and its graph link the customer to its sites, incidents, applications and XLA clauses.

Foundry IQ

The Zava-SD-Contracts agent retrieves the applicable clause from the Service agreements knowledge base, cites the signed agreement and computes nothing.

Work IQ

Mail, Teams chats, meetings and files show what is already underway and who owns each case. That decides who receives the message.

Web IQ

Public customer announcements add context for the service review, without changing contractual conclusions.

SayAvoid
“Foundry IQ retrieves the clause and cites the agreement.”“The AI decides what we owe.”
“The credit is a Fabric measure.”“The agent calculates the penalty.”
“Work IQ shows who already acts.”“Work IQ reads everyone's mailbox.”
“Web IQ adds context, not obligations.”“The news changes the contract.”
“The same wording is bound in the ontology.”“The model summarised the contract.”

7. Challenge questions for the room

Deliberate traps. The expected answers are locked in by the test suite and by the Data Agent's verified few-shots.

Audience questionExpected answer
Litware is below 40% too. Do we owe a credit?No. Its contract carries no credit: a written remediation plan within 10 business days, due 2 October.
How was the Fabrikam credit worked out?5% of the 185,000 EUR monthly service fee, 9,250 EUR, under a 15% cap. Computed as a Fabric measure.
Why not put the calculation rule in the prompt?Because we would have two definitions of zero-touch and of the credit. The measure stays in Fabric, auditable.
A Fabrikam user is calling. Is a known incident already covering them?Yes: Louis Dubois, Lyon, a VIP, is covered by the open Sev2 major incident on VPN and Teams — the agent can answer without a human.
What broke zero-touch at Fabrikam?A Corporate VPN update at Lyon; the device-health tool failed upstream, so the agents escalated to humans.
Is Foundry live in this demo?No: the Foundry plane is staged. The figures are live, and the Data Agent answers “figures + contract” questions live from the ontology.
Does Work IQ read everyone's mailbox?No: it works with the signed-in user's permissions. Here the context is staged, and names only the two people who own the cases.
